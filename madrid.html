Founded in 1902, Real Madrid is one of the most successful football clubs in the world. Known for its winning mentality and rich history, the club has won countless domestic and international trophies, making it a global football powerhouse.

Santiago Bernabéu

The Santiago Bernabéu is the legendary home of Real Madrid. Located in Madrid, Spain, it is one of football's most iconic stadiums, famous for its incredible atmosphere and hosting some of the sport's greatest matches.
